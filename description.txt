Adds swords inspired by the gem staves. They also have unique attacks (ex. Sapphire Longsword - Hitting an enemy 3 times makes an icicle drop on them). Try them out!

Currently adds 8 new swords:
Pre-Hardmode:
- Diamond Greatsword
- Ruby Greatsword
- Emerald Longsword
- Sapphire Longsword
- Topaz Blade
- Amethyst Blade
- Amber Blade
Hardmode:
- Greater Amethyst Blade
Planned features:
- More hardmode sword variants
